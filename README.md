## Hello, World

I am Xiao Weiqi, a Cybersecurity undergraduate interested in AI Agent systems, RAG, and graph learning.

My favorite kind of project is the one where an LLM stops being just a chat box and starts becoming a dependable worker: it can plan, call tools, remember preferences, stream progress, check evidence, and say "I do not know enough yet" when the knowledge boundary is reached.

Right now I am exploring:

- AI Agent systems with planning, tool calling, memory, MCP, and SSE streaming
- RAG pipelines with relevance judgment, local evidence, and external retrieval fallback
- Graph deep learning for subgraph matching and pattern densest subgraph mining
- Multimodal learning, federated learning, and security-aware Agent workflows

| Multi-Agent Travel Assistant | Project Lead | A Manager-Workers travel Agent system that turns fuzzy travel intent into verified itineraries, route planning, ticket search, and local suggestions.
